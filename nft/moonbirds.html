📖 Overview

Moonbirds is a collection of 10,000 pixel-art owl profile-picture (PFP) NFTs on Ethereum (ERC-721), launched in mid-April 2022 by PROOF, the company behind the members-only PROOF Collective founded by internet entrepreneur Kevin Rose. Minting cost 2.5 ETH (roughly $7,000 at the time) and access to the public sale ran through a raffle that required 2.5 ETH in a registered wallet; of the 10,000 supply, 2,000 were reserved for PROOF Collective members (two per member), 7,875 went to the public raffle, and 125 were held back by PROOF for collaborations and marketing. The launch was one of the largest of the 2022 cycle: about $280M in total sales volume within two days ($290M within four), briefly putting Moonbirds ahead of Bored Ape Yacht Club and Mutant Ape Yacht Club in trading volume.

The collection introduced "nesting," a staking-style mechanic in which holders lock a Moonbird (making it unsellable while nested) to accrue tier upgrades from Straw up to Diamond Nest (0–180 days) and earn holder rewards such as trait-based drops and the Oddities companion collection. The artwork is rendered "in-chain" — images are output directly from the Ethereum smart contracts rather than stored on IPFS. In August 2022 Rose moved Moonbirds and Oddities art to a CC0 public-domain license, and the same month PROOF raised a $50M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), with participation from Seven Seven Six, Collab+Currency, Flamingo DAO, SV Angel and VaynerFund (Alexis Ohanian's Seven Seven Six had already invested $10M in April 2022).

Each Moonbird combines traits across eight published categories (Background, Beak, Body, Eyes, Eyewear, Feathers, Headwear, Outerwear); one catalog source counts 126 distinct trait values in total, with individual birds carrying 3–8 traits. The rarest tier is four "grail" variants — Cosmic, Enlightened, Glitch and Jade — with only 6 of each (24 birds total), each paired with a matching exclusive background (Cosmic Purple, Enlightened Purple, Glitch Red, Jade Green). Ownership of the brand has changed twice: Yuga Labs acquired PROOF (Moonbirds, Oddities, Grails) on February 16, 2024, then sold the Moonbirds IP — together with Oddities and Mythics — to web3 gaming startup Orange Cap Games in a deal announced May 30, 2025.

🌍 Cultural significance

Moonbirds was the defining launch of the late-cycle 2022 NFT market: roughly $280M in volume in its first two days made it briefly the top-trading collection, flipping BAYC and MAYC, and its "nesting" mechanic popularized soft-staking for PFP projects. Its cultural arc also tracks the market's turbulence — COO Ryan Carson quit nine days after launch to start an NFT fund (taking ~$1M in Moonbirds with him), drawing community backlash; the August 2022 switch to CC0 divided holders who felt exclusive image rights had been removed unilaterally; and the a16z-led $50M raise the same month made PROOF one of the best-capitalized NFT startups. The most famous single purchase was corporate rather than celebrity: metaverse firm The Sandbox bought Moonbird #2642 for 350 ETH (~$1M). Governance experiments included the Lunar Society DAO (February 2023, seeded with $2M from PROOF's treasury). After the market downturn, Yuga Labs absorbed PROOF and Moonbirds in February 2024, then sold the IP to Orange Cap Games in May 2025 — a hand-off that reportedly triggered a sharp floor-price rally and repositioned the owls toward gaming.
