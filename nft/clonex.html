📖 Overview

CLONE X — X TAKASHI MURAKAMI (commonly "CloneX") is a collection of 20,000 algorithmically generated 3D avatar NFTs on Ethereum, created by digital-fashion studio RTFKT with Japanese contemporary artist Takashi Murakami. Unlike flat PFP projects, each Clone was conceived as a metaverse-ready identity: holders received fully rigged 3D character files usable in games, AR filters, and virtual environments, and Murakami contributed signature motifs (flowers, smiley mouths, "drip" elements) as generative traits. Rarity is anchored by eight "DNA" types, from Human (50%) down to Alien (0.15%).

The launch ran in late November 2021: a presale of 11,133 tokens for existing RTFKT NFT holders at 0.05 ETH opened November 27, with the public sale (2 ETH, initially structured as a Dutch auction from 3 ETH) planned for November 29 but delayed a day after RTFKT's site buckled under demand and bot attacks; the collection nonetheless sold out. Two weeks later, on December 13, 2021, Nike acquired RTFKT — terms undisclosed — instantly making CloneX the centerpiece of Nike's web3 strategy and cementing its blue-chip status through 2022, when floor prices peaked in the high teens of ETH.

The project's later arc is a cautionary tale. Nike announced in December 2024 that it would wind down RTFKT operations by end of January 2025. On April 24, 2025, CloneX and Animus artwork briefly went dark across marketplaces after Cloudflare downgraded RTFKT's account, displaying a "content restricted" notice; former RTFKT tech lead Samuel Cardillo subsequently migrated the artwork to the permanent-storage network Arweave. In parallel, a class action filed in 2025 sought over $5 million from Nike, characterizing the RTFKT shutdown as a "soft rug pull." Co-founder Benoit Pagotto died in October 2025 at age 41.

🌍 Cultural significance

CloneX marked Takashi Murakami's full entry into NFTs and became the template for fashion-brand web3 plays: Nike's December 13, 2021 acquisition of RTFKT — two weeks after mint — was read industry-wide as legacy fashion's endorsement of digital collectibles, and CloneX avatars anchored Nike/RTFKT's subsequent ecosystem (MNLTH/Cryptokicks sneaker NFTs, physical "forging" of apparel, CloneX Chains jewelry with Crown Collection). The collection crossed into the traditional art world in May 2022 when Gagosian's "An Arrow through History" (976/980 Madison Avenue, New York — Murakami's first solo New York show at the gallery since 2014) exhibited paintings and sculptures based on CloneX alongside the gallery's first VR experience, built with RTFKT and Oncyber. The collection is also cited for inclusivity in generative design (e.g., vitiligo skin variants). Its decline — Nike's December 2024 wind-down of RTFKT, the April 2025 Cloudflare outage that temporarily blanked the artwork (prompting migration to Arweave), and a class action calling the shutdown a "soft rug pull" — made CloneX a reference case in debates over corporate custodianship of NFT projects and off-chain asset fragility.

Famous rare traits

  • Alien DNA ↗ (0.15% (= 30 of 20,000)) — Rarest DNA type; despite scarcity, Aliens have not commanded the collection's top sales — the priciest early Alien sale was 88.88 ETH (Dec 2021)
  • Murakami DNA ↗ (0.5% (= 100 of 20,000)) — Fully Murakami-designed clones; the most coveted DNA in practice — the record 450 ETH sale (#4594) was a Murakami DNA clone. Also counted among the Murakami Drip traits
  • Undead DNA ↗ (0.6% (= 120 of 20,000)) — Third-rarest DNA type
  • Reptile DNA ↗ (1.25% (= 250 of 20,000) · 0.01% of supply) — Fourth-rarest DNA type
  • Murakami Drip traits ↗ (39 distinct traits · 0.19% of supply) — Traits designed by Takashi Murakami (eyes, mouths, helmets, outfits); roughly 15% of all clones carry at least one Murakami Drip trait
  • White Octopus (WHT-OCTOPUS) hair ↗ (~0.43%) — Rare hair trait on record-sale CloneX #4594
