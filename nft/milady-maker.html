📖 Overview

Milady Maker is a collection of 10,000 generative ERC-721 profile-picture NFTs on Ethereum, created by Remilia Corporation (also styled Remilia Collective) and launched on August 25, 2021. The avatars are drawn in a self-described "neochibi" anime style inspired by Y2K Tokyo street-fashion tribes; every Milady is procedurally assembled from cosmetics themed around five style "cores" — Gyaru, Prep, Lolita, Hypebeast, and Harajuku (with 15 core combinations in the metadata). The mint used tiered pricing that discounted bulk purchases, and the collection took roughly eight months to mint out, completing on April 19, 2022 — a slow start for what later became one of the most traded PFP collections on Ethereum. The artwork is copylefted under the Viral Public License.

Milady Maker's signature mechanic is the "drip score": instead of ranking purely by trait scarcity, each cosmetic is scored on rarity weighted by the prominence of its slot, with bonus points for matching hat/shirt themes ("costume complete" badges). Every token carries an on-chain-referenced Drip Score (observed range 12–67) and a letter Drip Grade from Normal/C/B/A/S up to SS (533 of 10,000 are SS-drip). The project frames this as valuing stylistic coherence over raw rarity. Direction is credited to Charlotte Fang (Remilia's founder, widely reported to be Krishna Okhandiar), with the visual style executed by a digital painter credited in Remilia's design notes as Milady Sonora (site credits list Yuyu, Sprite, and Charlotte Fang as artists).

The collection's trajectory has been unusually volatile even by NFT standards: a founder scandal in May 2022 cut the floor by more than half, an Elon Musk meme tweet in May 2023 roughly doubled it within hours, and Vitalik Buterin's adoption of a Milady profile picture in January 2025 triggered another major rally. Through these swings Milady became the anchor of a sprawling ecosystem — derivative collections (Redacted Remilio Babies, Bonkler, community-made Pixelady), memecoins (LADYS, the Milady Cult Coin/CULT), and an internet-subculture identity ("network spirituality") that has kept it culturally prominent well past the 2021 PFP cycle.

🌍 Cultural significance

Milady Maker is as famous for its subculture as for its art. In May 2022, DefiLlama developer 0xngmi linked founder Charlotte Fang to the pseudonymous "Miya" account, which had posted racist and homophobic content and been tied to an alleged online "suicide cult"; Fang admitted to running the account, apologized, and announced departure from the team — the floor fell over 55% to roughly $454 — though Fang later described the exit as "performative" and resumed open leadership, recasting Miya as collaborative satirical performance art under Remilia's "post-authorship" doctrine. The collection rebounded into an unlikely blue chip: on May 10–11, 2023 Elon Musk tweeted a Milady meme captioned "There is no meme. I love you," sending the floor from ~3.75 ETH to over 7 ETH within hours and the unaffiliated LADYS memecoin up over 5,000%; in January 2025 Vitalik Buterin bought Milady #9286 (5.82 ETH) and made it his profile picture, driving another ~40%+ floor surge. Around the collection grew the "network spirituality" aesthetic movement, official derivatives (Redacted Remilio Babies, Bonkler), community spin-offs (Pixelady), and the CULT token (December 2024), whose presale reportedly raised $20 million in six hours — making Milady one of the few 2021-vintage PFP projects to remain culturally central through 2026.

Famous rare traits

  • Eth Necklace (Necklace) (14) — The collection's most famous rare trait; worn by Milady #7489, the reported record sale. Exactly 14 of 10,000 per the official metadata.
  • Migoko Hat (Hat) (21) — Rarest hat in the collection.
  • Halo (Hat) (34) — Second-rarest hat; one of the most recognizable grails.
  • Moakleys (Glasses) (40) — Rarest glasses (a mock-Oakleys design); other notable rares: Malenciaga Neon shirt (49), Tennis Outfit shirt (54), Celine Dog earring (63).
  • Alien (Race) (134) — Rarest skin type; Black race (341) is the second-rarest, deliberately made scarce per Remilia's design notes.
  • SS Drip Grade (533) — Top tier of the drip-score system; only two tokens (#5930 and #6844) reach the maximum Drip Score of 67, and a single token (#1346) fills the most trait slots (12 cosmetics).
