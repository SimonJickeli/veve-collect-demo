📖 Overview

Mad Lads is a 10,000-piece profile-picture collection on Solana — roughly 9,000 male "Lads" and 1,000 female "Lassies" — drawn by artist Yann Penno in a style commonly described as anime-meets-Peaky-Blinders. It was created by Coral (later rebranded Backpack), the developer of the Anchor framework and the Backpack wallet, led by Armani Ferrante and Tristan Yver. Mad Lads was the first collection of "xNFTs" (executable NFTs): tokens carrying embedded code that runs as a native app inside the Backpack wallet, enabling token-gated chats, staking for soulbound "gold," collection locking, and direct-to-wallet claims.

The launch became part of the collection's lore. Madlist (allowlist) claims opened February 28, 2023; the mint was announced April 11 and priced at 6.9 SOL. The April 20, 2023 public mint was hit by DDoS attacks, extortion threats, and hundreds of thousands of bogus bot transactions, forcing a 24-hour postponement. On the April 21 relaunch the team routed humans to the real mint and bots to a decoy "honeypot" mint contract, which trapped roughly $250,000 in SOL from bots — refunded within 24 hours. The collection sold out, and within days secondary trading records fell: a peak of about $8.2 million in 24-hour volume, nearly triple Bored Ape Yacht Club's over the same span, with Solana's daily NFT volume exceeding 50% of Ethereum's for the first time. Artwork was revealed through a built-in game in which holders collectively "defeated" the Mad King inside their NFTs.

Arriving months after the FTX collapse and weeks after DeGods and y00ts left Solana, Mad Lads is widely credited with reviving the Solana NFT market — Ferrante described the moment as the space needing "a new heartbeat." The floor peaked at 229.425 SOL (~$16,000) in early December 2023 amid Backpack Exchange early-access perks; lifetime secondary volume passed $200 million (≈3.51M SOL through 2024), making it Solana's leading collection by market cap and volume for extended periods.

🌍 Cultural significance

Mad Lads is credited with restoring confidence in Solana NFTs at the ecosystem's lowest point — post-FTX and immediately after DeGods/y00ts migrated away — and with proving the xNFT concept of NFTs as executable apps. In its first days it out-traded every Solana and Ethereum collection, and in April 2023 a Mad Lads trading incentive briefly let Tensor flip Magic Eden in Solana marketplace volume. Holding a Lad became a yield position: holders got first-wave access to Backpack Exchange (November 2023) and the collection became the most-airdropped on Solana, receiving allocations from Pyth Network, Wormhole, Dymension, and dozens of other projects, which drove the December 2023 floor peak above 200 SOL. Community identity centers on the mottos "WAO" (We Are One) and "Fock It," global meetups (Tokyo, Seoul, Manila, Istanbul, Vietnam, and elsewhere), a sponsored extreme-sports "Roster," a Solana Mobile partnership engraving Lads onto Saga devices, and honorary 1/1s awarded to public figures — Iggy Azalea (an honorary Lassie, dubbed "Mother" by the community), Haliey "Hawk Tuah" Welch, and Meltem Demirors. Solana co-founder Anatoly Yakovenko publicly endorsed the launch, calling xNFTs a distribution bridge to mass consumer audiences.

Famous rare traits

  • King type — the "Mad King" (Mad Lad #8420) (1) — The collection's only 1/1 character and rarity rank #1: it uniquely combines six 1-of-1 traits — Mad Crown (hat), Madness (eyes), Mad Armor (clothing), Royal (expression), and Royal Rug (background). The Mad King was the boss holders collectively defeated in the xNFT reveal game.
  • Skull type ("Mad Skulls") (93) — Undead skull-faced Lads; noted from mint week as commanding large premiums.
  • Galaxy type (403) — Celestial-skinned Lads; the sub-family includes 1/1s Galaxy Cloche (hat) and Galaxy Wolfsbane Dress (clothing), plus Galaxy Beret hair (33).
  • Golden hand items (6–16 each) — Gold weapon/prop variants: Golden Cigarette (6), Golden Peacemaker (6), Golden Tommygun (6), Golden Broomhandle (7), Golden Cigar (16).
  • Female ("Lassies") (969) — Female-gender Lads per HowRare.is (marketed as 1,000 Lassies incl. specials); consistently trade above male counterparts.
  • Ultra-rare clothing (3–4 each) — Evangelion (3), Curie Gown (3), Mink Coat (4); Maid Dress (12) and Cartwheel hat (16) are also sub-20 traits.
