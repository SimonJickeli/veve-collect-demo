📖 Overview

CryptoPunks is a collection of 10,000 algorithmically generated 24x24-pixel characters on Ethereum, released by the two-person studio Larva Labs in June 2017. It is one of the earliest non-fungible token projects on Ethereum and predates the ERC-721 standard, which it is credited with inspiring; the collection uses its own custom contract with a built-in marketplace. The artwork draws on the London punk scene, the cyberpunk movement, and Daft Punk. At launch the punks were free to claim (gas only); claiming was slow for the first week until a June 2017 Mashable article, after which the remaining supply was claimed within about a day. Larva Labs' founders claimed 1,000 punks for themselves before the public release.

The original 2017 contract contained a marketplace bug that let a buyer reclaim the ETH they had paid, so Larva Labs deployed a fixed contract on June 23, 2017 and re-issued all 10,000 punks to their existing owners; this "V2" contract (0xb47e3cd837dDF8e4c57F05d70Ab865de6e193BBB) is the canonical collection today, while the abandoned V1 tokens later resurfaced as a separate wrapped collection. Each punk is one of five types — 6,039 Male, 3,840 Female, 88 Zombie, 24 Ape, and 9 Alien — and carries zero to seven of 87 possible accessories (hats, hair, glasses, beards, etc.). Rarity is driven by type, by scarce accessories, and by unusual attribute counts.

Ownership of the IP has changed hands twice: Yuga Labs (creator of Bored Ape Yacht Club) acquired the CryptoPunks and Meebits IP from Larva Labs on March 11, 2022 and granted commercial rights to individual holders; in May 2025 Yuga Labs sold the CryptoPunks IP to the nonprofit Infinite Node Foundation (NODE), which stewards the collection and runs museum-placement programs.

🌍 Cultural significance

CryptoPunks is widely treated as the archetype of NFT collectibles: an inspiration for the ERC-721 standard, a catalyst of the 2021 NFT boom, and the profile-picture status symbol of that era. Visa bought CryptoPunk #7610 for 49.5 ETH (~$150,000) in August 2021, a purchase credited with triggering a record sales run, and celebrity owners have included Jay-Z, Serena Williams (a gift from husband Alexis Ohanian), and other high-profile figures. Punks were the first NFTs sold in marquee evening sales at both Christie's ($16.96M for nine punks, May 2021) and Sotheby's ($11.75M for #7523, June 2021). Through the Punks Legacy Project, punks entered permanent museum collections — #305 at the ICA Miami (December 2022) and #110 at the Centre Pompidou in Paris (February 2023) — and Wikipedia records MoMA adding ten CryptoPunks in December 2025. The infamous October 2021 "sale" of punk #9998 for $532M was a flash-loan self-purchase and is excluded from legitimate sale records.

Famous rare traits

  • Alien ↗ (9 · 0.09% of supply) — Rarest type; aliens account for most of the top all-time sales (#5822, #7804, #3100, #7523)
  • Ape ↗ (24 · 0.24% of supply) — Second-rarest type (e.g. #4156, sold for 2,500 ETH in Dec 2021)
  • Zombie ↗ (88 · 0.88% of supply) — Third-rarest type
  • Beanie ↗ (44 · 0.44% of supply) — Rarest accessory in the collection
  • 7 attributes ↗ (1 · 0.01% of supply) — Only one punk has the maximum seven accessories (widely identified as #8348, which stacks Big Beard, Buck Teeth, and more)
  • 0 attributes ↗ (8 · 0.08% of supply) — Only eight punks have no accessories at all; a distinct rarity niche
