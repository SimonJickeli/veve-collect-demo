📖 Overview

Bored Ape Yacht Club (BAYC) is a collection of 10,000 procedurally generated profile-picture NFTs depicting cartoon apes, minted as ERC-721 tokens on Ethereum (contract 0xBC4CA0EdA7647A8aB7C2061c2E118A18a936f13D). It launched in late April 2021 at a mint price of 0.08 ETH (~$190) and sold out within about 12 hours. The collection's fiction casts each ape as a member of a swamp-side club for apes grown "bored" after crypto wealth; the token itself functions as a membership card, originally gating perks such as a collaborative-graffiti "bathroom." Unusually for the era, Yuga Labs granted holders commercial-use rights to their individual ape images, which enabled holder-run brands, music projects, and merchandise.

BAYC was created by Yuga Labs, founded in early 2021 by writers Greg Solano ("Gargamel") and Wylie Aronow ("Gordon Goner") together with engineers Zeshan Ali ("No Sass") and Kerem Atalay ("Emperor Tomato Ketchup"); the founders operated pseudonymously until their identities were made public in February 2022. The lead artist on the collection was the Asian-American illustrator known as All Seeing Seneca. Each ape is assembled from seven trait categories (background, fur, eyes, mouth, hat, clothes, earring) drawn from a pool the project describes as over 170 possible traits, with 4 to 7 traits per ape; trait scarcity drives large price differences between individual apes.

BAYC became the defining "PFP" collection of the 2021–22 NFT cycle. Its floor price peaked around 128 ETH (~$354,000) in May 2022, when Yuga Labs was valued at roughly $4 billion and had expanded the ecosystem with companion collections, the ApeCoin token (March 16, 2022), the acquisition of the CryptoPunks and Meebits IP from Larva Labs (March 2022), and the Otherside metaverse project (launched April 30, 2022, with Animoca Brands). In the subsequent bear market the floor fell roughly 90%, to about 13.4 ETH (~$40,000) by May 2024. Cumulative BAYC trading volume has exceeded $1 billion.

🌍 Cultural significance

BAYC became the mainstream face of the 2021–22 NFT boom and, later, of its bust. Celebrity owners included Justin Bieber, Eminem, Snoop Dogg, Paris Hilton, Jimmy Fallon, Gwyneth Paltrow, and Stephen Curry, and Eminem and Snoop Dogg performed as their apes in the 2022 "From the D 2 the LBC" music video; Adidas bought an ape in 2021 ahead of its metaverse push. Sotheby's legitimized the collection in traditional auction circles with the $24.39M "Ape In!" lot (September 2021) and the record $3.4M sale of #8817 (October 2021). Yuga Labs leveraged the brand into ApeCoin, the Otherside metaverse, and ownership of CryptoPunks and Meebits, reaching a ~$4B valuation in 2022. The project also drew the era's backlash: an SEC investigation opened in October 2022, a class-action suit filed in December 2022 (dismissed September 2025), phishing/theft incidents against holders, copycat collections, and a ~90% floor-price collapse by 2024 — making BAYC a standard case study in both NFT cultural adoption and speculative excess.
