📖 Overview

Bitcoin Puppets is a 10,001-piece profile-picture (PFP) collection inscribed on Bitcoin via the Ordinals protocol. The mint went live on January 3, 2024 on the inscription service InscribeNow (inscribenow.io) as a free mint (minters paid network/inscription fees) and minted out quickly, with some suspected bot activity. The images are deliberately crude, hand-drawn MS Paint monkey-like puppet characters — the collection is famously self-described with the meme that it was 'hand-drawn in MS Paint by a 12-year-old' — and the project explicitly offers 'no roadmap, no promises': the official site describes the work as pure art created 'with basic tools, lots of effort and passion.'

The creator is a pseudonymous, deliberately undoxxed artist known as Le Puppeteer Fou (X/Twitter: @lepuppeteerfou), also called 'The Puppeteer' or 'The Puppetmaster.' Before Bitcoin Puppets he created Lasogette, a 7,777-piece generative 'net-art' PFP collection on Ethereum, and O.P.I.U.M. (Ord Puppet Inu Undoxxed Millionaires), a 777-piece hand-drawn puppet collection free-minted on Bitcoin Ordinals on March 25, 2023, which is widely considered the direct predecessor of Bitcoin Puppets. The founding community traces back to friendships formed in the Wassies by Wassies Discord during the Ethereum NFT era, and community members contributed directly to trait design in both O.P.I.U.M. (submitting the text on puppets' signs) and Bitcoin Puppets.

The collection is released under the Viral Public License (VPL), a CC0-like copyleft that predates CC0-style NFT licensing and permits anyone to make derivatives and merchandise; the artist simultaneously holds a firm 'No-Collab Thesis' refusing official collaborations. Notable sub-groups include the 'Cursed Puppets' (a set of 21 glitched/fringe inscriptions listed by number on the official site) and the separately tracked 'Bitcoin Puppets Honoraries,' an ever-growing set of 1-of-1 puppets the artist creates as tributes to figures such as Ordinals creator Casey Rodarmor, DeeZe, ThreadGuy, and Ansem.

Market-wise, the collection's floor hit a then-record 0.178 BTC (~$11,141) on February 29, 2024 per Magic Eden data, then surged during the spring 2024 Ordinals run: on April 12, 2024 the floor reached ~0.46 BTC, and on April 22, 2024 (nftnow's publication date) it sat at 0.408 BTC (~$26,775), with the collection coming close to flipping NodeMonkes by market cap and floor around April 8, 2024. Weekly volume spiked again in late 2024 ($3.03M in one September 2024 week per crypto.news). The unaffiliated but community-adopted $PUPS memecoin ecosystem (BRC-20, bridged to Solana, migrated to Runes as PUPS•WORLD•PEACE at the April 2024 halving) rewarded Puppet holders via the Rune Pups airdrop of March 17, 2024. As of mid-2026, CoinGecko shows the floor around 0.0102 BTC (~$660-820) with roughly 6,021 holders across 10,001 items.

🌍 Cultural significance

Bitcoin Puppets became the definitive anti-establishment statement of the 2024 Ordinals wave. Where Ethereum PFP projects sold polished art, roadmaps, and 'utility,' the Puppets offered deliberately ugly MS Paint scrawl, a manifesto titled 'The Gospel of World Peace,' a middle-finger cursor easter egg on the official website, and the ironic community rallying cry 'send them to zero' — a rejection of floor-price obsession that reframed financial nihilism as community glue. The polarizing art was itself the filter: mockery from outsiders ('FUDders') only strengthened the cult, and holders flooded X/Twitter timelines with puppet imagery and world-peace messaging, making the puppet a recognizable meme persona of Bitcoin-native counterculture and, alongside NodeMonkes, the face of Ordinals' challenge to Ethereum blue chips (whose floors, like BAYC's, were collapsing at the same moment).

The trait lore is genuinely community-authored: the 'two chairs' t-shirt trait evolved from a community member's riff on Michael Saylor's 'how many chairs are you sitting on?' Bitcoin rhetoric (with a red/blue Matrix-pill twist by the artist), the 'pink pipe' references BennyTheDev's PIPE protocol ('trac pipe' joke), and the 'Coffee Time' mug immortalizes a community member's misprinted merchandise order. The VPL license spawned a large derivative ecosystem (Le Puppettes and many others), music albums, merch, and IRL meetups at ETH Denver, NFT.NYC, and Bitcoin 2024 Nashville — all without any official collaboration, by design.

⚠ Unverified / disputed claims (found during research but not confirmed — excluded from the article above):
  • The name 'LeoCrypto' as creator could not be confirmed in any source; every credible source attributes the collection to the pseudonymous Le Puppeteer Fou — treat 'LeoCrypto' as incorrect
  • Some aggregators (NFT Price Floor, CryptoRank-derived pages) claim an 'initial mint price of 0.1 BTC'; this is contradicted by the official site ('free mint sold out on inscribenow.io') and nftnow's reporting of a free mint on InscribeNow — one newsletter cites roughly $10 in mint-time costs (likely fees)
  • The claim that the artist 'is a 12-year-old' is the collection's self-deprecating meme description, not a verified biographical fact
  • Exact inscription-number range of the 10,001 inscriptions (rarity explorers were not accessible: Ordiscan/Magic Eden blocked automated fetching)
  • No public trait-category breakdown with distinct-value counts was found; marketplaces expose attribute filters but the category list and counts were not retrievable from accessible sources
  • No individual record sale (specific puppet, price, date) is reliably documented in accessible reporting; CryptoSlam shows per-item last sales around $8,800-10,500 (e.g. Bitcoin Puppet #6836 at $10,537) without dates, and an April 2024 holder tweet cited 3 puppets bought under $2k being 'worth $90k' — a valuation, not a sale
  • A reported April 2024 floor peak of ~0.44 BTC (~$30,000) and a peak market cap 'briefly touching almost $1B' (other aggregators say $760-800M) appeared in secondary commentary and could not be pinned to a primary data source; the best-verified peak-era data points are 0.46 BTC (April 12, 2024, quoted by nftnow) and 0.408 BTC / $26,775 (April 22, 2024, nftnow)
  • The X account @BitcoinPuppets and the site bitcoinpuppets.community present themselves as community-run; the only artist-confirmed handle is @lepuppeteerfou and the only official site is ordpuppetinuundoxxedmillionaires.com
  • The 'Bitcoin Puppets Honoraries' are tracked as a separate collection on Forbes/CoinGecko; exact honoraries supply is described only as 'ever-growing'
