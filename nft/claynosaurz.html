📖 Overview

Claynosaurz is a collection of 3D-animated, claymation-style dinosaur NFTs on Solana, created by a studio of animation-industry veterans. The core mint of 10,000 characters went live on November 26, 2022 and sold out (co-founder Nicholas Cabana says the launch "sold out and crashed the website" and raised about $1.3 million; trackers such as Moon.ly and Bitget News report a mint price of 10 SOL). A burn mechanic later expanded the genesis collection: 222 'Sardinha' items could be burned with a Claymaker into Giant Eggs that hatched into Dactyls, bringing the flagship collection to 10,222 characters across 7 species (Ankylo, Bronto, Dactyl, Raptor, Rex, Stego, Trice), per the official GitBook. A separate 2,000-piece expansion, 'Claynosaurz: The Call of Saga' (species Para and Spino, 1,000 each), was announced in April 2023 exclusively for Solana Mobile Saga phone users; it is its own collection and should not be conflated with the 10,222 genesis supply. Aggregators vary slightly on supply (HowRare indexes 10,000 core items; CryptoSlam shows 10,002; CoinGecko lists 10,232).

The studio was founded in late 2021 by animators Nicholas Cabana and Daniel Cabral, who serve as co-chief creative officers. Cabana started at Sony Animation & Imageworks and worked in VFX/creature animation at MPC, Image Engine, Rodeo FX and Framestore, with credits on Jurassic World, Paddington 2, Fantastic Beasts, X-Men: Days of Future Past and Suicide Squad (Annie Award nomination), plus animation-supervisor work on Game of Thrones and His Dark Materials. Cabral is a Brazilian animator who originally created the Claynosaurz characters for his son. The wider team draws artists from Illumination, DreamWorks, Sony, Framestore, Disney, Ubisoft, Netflix and others; Andrew Pelekis (CFA, formerly a director at Innovatus Capital Partners) is CEO, per the official docs and IQ.wiki.

Every Claynosaurz NFT is a fully animated 3D character rather than a static image — motion is literally a trait (walk cycles such as Gallop, Trot, Run and Idle), reflecting the founders' pitch that blockchain lets animators own and monetize 'a walk cycle, a gesture, an expression.' The brand positions itself as a community-owned transmedia IP: viral animated shorts on social media, large fan events (Lisbon, Los Angeles, New York, Paris, Annecy presence), a Gameloft mobile-game partnership announced September 12, 2024 (choose-your-own-adventure plus location-based mechanics), a merchandise shop, an expansion onto Sui with the 25,000-piece 'Popkins' collection, and in July 2026 the release of its first three animated micro-episodes (characters Flea, Bex, Trix and Milo in the world 'Claynotopia') on Amazon Prime Video, also available on Apple TV.

The trait system (verified via the HowRare index of the 10,000-item core collection) spans Species, Skin (10 values, rarest 'Apres' at 281), Color (8, rarest 'Mist' at 388), Motion (6, rarest 'Run' at 693), Mood (8), Background (6), Class (6: Warrior, Mystic, Stalker, Defender, Tracker, Mender), layer/on-off traits, and 14 one-of-one 'Ancients' — six golden 'Midas' dinos (one per species) plus eight named characters (Trevor, Travis, Trish, Tristan, Tyson, Tom, Tiffany, and 'Toly, Toby And Tony'). The official docs count 22 Ancients in total: 18 across the genesis collection (including Dactyl Ancients) and 4 in The Call of Saga. CoinGecko records an all-time-high floor of 97 SOL (April 3, 2023) and $5,843.88 (December 19, 2024); CryptoSlam's all-time top sale is Ancient Midas Bronto (#8755) at 537.5 SOL (~$116,139).

🌍 Cultural significance

Claynosaurz became one of the defining NFT brands of the post-2022 'NFT winter,' proving a Solana collection could grow while most PFP projects faded. Its soft, toy-like claymation aesthetic deliberately reads as mainstream family entertainment rather than crypto-native PFP culture, and the team framed the mint itself as a crowdfunding mechanism for an artist-owned IP — a story the animation trade press (Animation Magazine, AWN) has covered as a creator-economy success case, with Cabana keynoting the 2025 World Animation Summit and speaking at the VIEW Conference. The project claims over 40 industry awards for its animated content and built an audience of hundreds of thousands across social platforms through viral shorts.

Within the Solana ecosystem it functions as something of a mascot IP: the Call of Saga expansion was the flagship perk for Solana Mobile's Saga phone, an Ancient is named 'Toly, Toby And Tony,' and news events (the Gameloft deal in September 2024, the Amazon Prime Video debut in July 2026) repeatedly sent its floor price to the top of Solana NFT rankings — by July 2026 its market cap (~$19–20M) briefly surpassed Ethereum blue chips Milady Maker and Azuki. Its trajectory from NFT mint to Prime Video micro-series, mobile games and plush merchandise is frequently cited as the template for 'NFT-to-IP' brand building.
