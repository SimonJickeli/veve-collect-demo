📖 Overview

Azuki is a collection of 10,000 anime-styled avatar (PFP) NFTs on Ethereum, created by Los Angeles-based startup Chiru Labs and launched on January 12, 2022. The public sale used a Dutch auction starting at 1 ETH (set to decay 0.05 ETH every 20 minutes toward 0.15 ETH), but the 8,700 publicly offered tokens sold out in roughly four minutes at the full 1 ETH price, grossing about $29 million before the first price reduction could trigger; remaining supply went to a whitelist sale and the team. Holders gain membership in "The Garden," the project's community and brand universe, signified by a red bean motif.

Chiru Labs was founded by four pseudonymous builders — Zagabond (CEO, described as a former big-tech entrepreneur), 2pm.flow (CTO), location tba (engineer) and HoshiBoy (CPO) — with Arnold Tsang (alias Steamboy), a former Overwatch character artist, as art director. The ecosystem grew through the BEANZ companion collection (~19,900 tokens airdropped free to holders on March 31, 2022) and the Elementals collection (June 27, 2023), a 20,000-piece expansion that raised about $38 million in 15 minutes but triggered a community revolt because the artwork closely resembled the originals; Azuki's floor fell roughly 44% in the aftermath.

Each Azuki is generated from 12 trait categories (Type, Hair, Clothing, Eyes, Mouth, Offhand, Background, Ear, Face, Headgear, Neck, Special) spanning roughly 450 attribute values. The four Type tiers dominate valuation: Human (~90.2%), Blue (~4.4%), Red (~4.4%) and Spirit — only 97 exist. In 2024–2025 the project pivoted toward an "on-chain anime" strategy, launching AnimeChain (an Arbitrum-based L2) and the ANIME token on January 23, 2025, with 37.5% of the 10 billion supply allocated to the Azuki community.

🌍 Cultural significance

Azuki became the defining anime-aesthetic NFT brand of the 2021–22 cycle, briefly rivaling Bored Ape Yacht Club in floor price and establishing that a non-Western art style could anchor a blue-chip collection. Its history is marked by sharp swings driven by its own team: in May 2022 founder Zagabond's blog post "A Builder's Journey" revealed he had abandoned three earlier NFT projects (CryptoPhunks, Tendies, CryptoZunks), roughly halving the floor from ~19–21 ETH to ~10–11 ETH in a day; in June 2023 the Elementals mint raised ~$38M in 15 minutes but the near-identical artwork was seen as diluting the brand, cutting Azuki's floor ~44%. The project also contributed the open Physical Backed Token (PBT) standard, demonstrated with its October 2022 golden-skateboard auction, and pivoted into anime media infrastructure with AnimeChain (Arbitrum L2) and the ANIME token (January 23, 2025, 37.5% of supply to the Azuki community) — an attempt to convert a PFP collection into an "open anime" entertainment ecosystem. No celebrity ownership claims could be verified for this page.

Famous rare traits

  • Spirit (Type) (97) — Rarest of the four Type tiers (0.97%); Spirits average far above collection floor and include the record-sale pieces. Other tiers: Human ~90.18%, Blue ~4.44%, Red ~4.41%.
  • Golden Cat Kigurumi (Clothing) (5) — Rarest of the golden kigurumi series (cat, sloth, red panda, frog); the single scarcest documented clothing trait.
  • Golden Zanbato (Offhand) (11) — Rarest golden offhand weapon; other golden offhands: Golden Sheathed Katana (12), Golden Monkey King Staff (12), Golden Katana (12).
  • Spirit Long / Spirit Bob (Hair) (12 each) — Rarest hairstyles, exclusive to Spirit Azukis; Spirit Ponytail (15), Spirit Fluffy (15), Spirit Goddess (20).
  • Red Bean Earring (Ear) (14) — Bean-motif earrings referencing the project's azuki-bean branding; Blue Bean Earring has 16.
  • Golden attributes (any slot) (~313) — Per DappRadar's valuation study, 313 Azukis carry a golden item/neck/clothing/offhand trait; they sold for ~28 ETH on average vs ~8 ETH for non-golden at the time of the study.
