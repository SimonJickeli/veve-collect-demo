📖 Overview

Cool Cats is a collection of 9,999 randomly generated ERC-721 profile-picture (PFP) NFTs on Ethereum, each depicting "Blue Cat" — a cartoon character created by artist Colin Egan (Clon) roughly a decade before the drop (Animoca Brands dates the character to 2013). The collection launched on July 1, 2021 at a mint price of 0.06 ETH; after a slow first hour the team cut the price to 0.02 ETH (refunding early minters the difference), and the collection sold out within about eight hours. Each cat is assembled from four trait categories — body, hat, face, and shirt/outfit — drawn from a pool the team described as offering over 300,000 possible combinations.

Rarity is governed by a points-based "coolness" system: every trait carries points, and each cat's total (3–10 points) places it in one of four bands — Cool (3–4 pts, 4,599 cats), Wild (5–6 pts, 3,000), Classy (7–8 pts, 1,750) and Exotic (9–10 pts, ~650) — each split into two sub-tiers (cool_1 through exotic_2). Cool Cats became one of the archetypal "blue-chip" PFP collections of the 2021 NFT boom, following the CryptoPunks/Bored Ape playbook of a fixed-supply avatar set with community-driven value, and later expanded into a gamified ecosystem ("Cooltopia") with an ERC-20 token (MILK) and a 19,999-piece companion collection, Cool Pets (February 2022).

By 2025 the collection had generated over US$410 million in lifetime trading volume, ranking around 19th among all NFT collections by lifetime sales, and in August 2025 Animoca Brands and Coin Operated Group completed a strategic investment giving them a majority stake in Cool Cats Group.

🌍 Cultural significance

Cool Cats' cultural breakout came days after launch, when Mike Tyson set a Cool Cat as his Twitter profile picture (about eight days post-mint), an endorsement widely credited with accelerating the collection's rise; Steve Aoki is also a documented holder, and NFTevening additionally names Reese Witherspoon, Marc Benioff, Alexis Ohanian and Puma among buyers. The project partnered with TIME magazine in 2021, signed with Hollywood talent agency CAA in March 2022 for representation across licensing and media, and on November 23, 2023 became the first NFT collection featured in the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, with a balloon of Blue Cat and his milk-carton companion Chugs seen by an estimated 28.5 million viewers. Alongside CryptoPunks, Bored Apes, Doodles and Pudgy Penguins, Cool Cats is regularly cited as one of the definitive PFP collections of the 2021 cycle; its ecosystem expanded with the MILK token and Cooltopia gamified economy and the Cool Pets companion drop (19,999 NFTs, February 2022). After the 2022 downturn the collection's floor fell far below its roughly 14–19 ETH late-2021/early-2022 peak, and in August 2025 Animoca Brands and Coin Operated Group took a majority stake in Cool Cats Group, citing over $410M in lifetime volume.

Famous rare traits

  • Hidden 1/1 cats ↗ (9 · 0.09% of supply) — Nine one-of-one cats secretly created by Clon and xtremetom without telling the rest of the team (per OpenSea's own description); they include the zombie Cool Cat #1490 and the robot-themed Cool Cat #3330, and account for three of the collection's four highest early sales
  • Exotic tier (9–10 points) ↗ (~650 · 6.50% of supply) — Top coolness band; sources give 649–650 of 9,999
  • Exotic 2 sub-tier ↗ (<150 · 1.50% of supply) — The rarer half of the Exotic band — fewer than 150 cats of 9,999
