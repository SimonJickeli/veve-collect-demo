📖 Overview

Mutant Ape Yacht Club (MAYC) is an Ethereum NFT collection of up to 20,000 "mutant" apes created by Yuga Labs as the first direct expansion of the Bored Ape Yacht Club (BAYC). It launched on August 28, 2021 in two halves: 10,000 Mutant Apes were sold to the public in a surprise Dutch auction that began at 3 ETH (~$10,000) and sold out in under an hour, grossing about $96 million; the other 10,000 could only be created by existing BAYC holders, who were airdropped 10,000 "Mutant Serum" vials (7,500 M1, 2,492 M2, and 8 M3 "Mega Mutant" serums). Applying a serum to a Bored Ape burns the serum and mints a new Mutant Ape — M1 and M2 mutants are warped versions that retain the original ape's traits, while the eight M3 serums produce entirely new one-of-a-kind "Mega Mutants."

The collection was framed by Yuga Labs as a way to bring newcomers into the BAYC membership ecosystem at a lower price point while rewarding existing holders with a free, sellable asset, and it had been on the BAYC roadmap since the original 2021 launch. As of mid-2026 roughly 19,566 of the maximum 20,000 mutants have been minted (some serums remain unused), held by about 12,000 addresses. MAYC holders received full ecosystem benefits, including 2,042 ApeCoin (APE) per mutant in the March 2022 airdrop (versus 10,094 per Bored Ape).

MAYC became one of the highest-volume NFT collections of the 2021–22 cycle, with its floor price peaking around 40 ETH (~$116,000) in April–May 2022 before falling more than 95% in the subsequent market decline. Its serum/mutation mechanic — burning a derivative token against an existing NFT to mint a new one — was widely imitated and is regarded as a landmark in NFT "expansion collection" design.

🌍 Cultural significance

MAYC's launch was itself a cultural event: $96 million raised in under an hour made it one of the largest NFT drops to that point, and the serum airdrop pioneered the now-standard pattern of rewarding holders with a burnable derivative that mints a new asset. The collection functioned as the "second tier" of the most influential NFT membership brand of the era — MAYC holders received the March 2022 ApeCoin airdrop (2,042 APE each) and were included in subsequent Yuga ecosystem benefits — and its floor briefly reached ~40 ETH (~$116,000) at the April–May 2022 peak. Reported celebrity holders include Snoop Dogg (who assembled a full BAYC/MAYC set including M1 and M2 mutants), Shaquille O'Neal, and Lil Baby, and the theatrical Twitter Spaces "reveal parties" for multi-million-dollar Mega Mutant serum burns (notably Mega Trippy in February 2022) became emblematic of the 2021–22 NFT bull market. Like the wider market, MAYC later fell more than 95% from its price peak, but it remains among the highest all-time-volume NFT collections and a fixture of BAYC-ecosystem lore.

Famous rare traits

  • Mega Mutant (M3) ↗ (13 · 0.07% of supply) — The collection's grail tier: 8 created by burning the 8 airdropped M3 Mega Mutant serums (token IDs 30000+) plus 5 included in the 10,000-item public mint. Each Mega is a fully unique design carrying a 'Name' trait rather than standard trait combinations.
  • Mega Trippy (MAYC #30005) ↗ (1 · 0.01% of supply) — Created 2022-02 by applying an M3 serum (bought for 999 ETH, ~$3.8M) to BAYC #5235; holds the all-time MAYC sale record (2,300 ETH, Aug 2022).
  • Mega Demon (MAYC #4849) ↗ (1 · 0.01% of supply) — Public-mint Mega; first headline MAYC sale at 350 ETH one day after launch.
  • M2 mutants ↗ (max 2,492 · 12.74% of supply) — Only 2,492 M2 serums were airdropped, so M2-trait mutants are the rare mid-tier above the up-to-7,500 M1 mutants; individual M2 trait variants routinely fall under 1% of the collection.
  • Earring: None ↗ (~70% of collection · 0.36% of supply) — Earrings are the most skewed category — the 12 actual earring values are split across only ~30% of mutants.
