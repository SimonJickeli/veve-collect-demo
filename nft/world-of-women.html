📖 Overview

World of Women (WoW) is a collection of 10,000 generative profile-picture NFTs (ERC-721) on Ethereum, launched on July 27, 2021 at a mint price of 0.07 ETH and sold out within its first day. Every layer was hand-drawn by artist Yam Karkai — roughly 200 individual drawings made over about two months, randomly combined into portraits of diverse women — with artwork hosted on IPFS. The project's stated purpose was to counter the near-total absence of women in early NFT/PFP culture and to fund female artists through the WoW Fund and later the WoW Foundation.

The founding team is Yam Karkai (artist and public face; a former acting student in Paris with a background in illustration, scriptwriting and translation) together with co-founders Raphaël Malavieille (project management; Karkai's partner), Toomaie (generative-art developer) and the pseudonymous BBA. Karkai deliberately designed a rounded "base woman" who could read as multiple ethnicities, named the 14 skin tones respectfully (an approach inspired by Fenty Beauty's shade range), and excluded imagery with religious or appropriation risk from the generative layers.

WoW became the defining female-led "blue chip" of the 2021–22 NFT cycle: it was signed by Guy Oseary (manager of Madonna, U2 and BAYC) for mainstream representation, struck a film/TV development deal with Reese Witherspoon's Hello Sunshine (February 17, 2022), received a $25M five-year commitment from The Sandbox to create the WoW Foundation (announced February–March 2022), and made auction history at Christie's London in March 2022. A companion collection, World of Women Galaxy (22,222 NFTs, including 16 hand-drawn 1/1 "Champions and Guardians"), launched March 26, 2022 and generated roughly $79M in a day.

🌍 Cultural significance

World of Women is widely credited as the first female-created NFT collection to reach blue-chip status, and it became the emblem of the push for representation in a space then dominated by male-coded PFPs. Early boosts came from Gary Vaynerchuk (whose Instagram endorsement drove a wave of buying) and collectors including Logan Paul and Steve Aoki; celebrity holders and supporters came to include Reese Witherspoon, Eva Longoria and Shonda Rhimes. In January–March 2022 the project stacked mainstream milestones in quick succession: representation by Guy Oseary, the Hello Sunshine partnership to adapt WoW characters into film and TV (announced February 17, 2022), The Sandbox's $25M WoW Foundation grant funding 3D avatars, a WoW Museum and Web3 education for women, features tied to Billboard's Women in Music, and the record Christie's London sale. The WoW Galaxy expansion ($79M in first-day sales, March 2022) and partnerships with brands from FC Barcelona and Mastercard to Sephora and Jazwares extended the IP beyond crypto; like most 2021-era PFPs, market prices later fell far from their 2022 peaks, but WoW remains the reference case for women-led NFT projects.

Famous rare traits

  • Heterochromia Straight (Eyes) ↗ (77 · 0.77% of supply) — Rarest reported trait value in the collection — two different-colored eyes, straight gaze (0.77%).
  • Resting Butterfly (Face Accessories) ↗ (83 · 0.83% of supply) — A butterfly resting on the face; among the scarcest accessory values.
  • Night Goddess (Skin Tone) ↗ (85 · 0.85% of supply) — Rarest of the 14 skin tones (0.85%); the marquee 'grail' skin comparable to CryptoPunks' Alien tier within WoW.
  • Heterochromia Eye Roll (Eyes) ↗ (85 · 0.85% of supply) — Second heterochromia variant, eyes rolled upward.
  • Red Blue Bolt (Facial Features) ↗ (97 · 0.97% of supply) — Lightning-bolt face marking in red/blue; rarest facial-feature value reported.
  • Naiade (Clothes) ↗ (98 · 0.98% of supply) — Rarest clothing value reported by rarity trackers.
