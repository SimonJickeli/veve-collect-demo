📖 Overview

NodeMonkes is a collection of 10,000 pixel-art monkey avatars inscribed directly onto individual satoshis on the Bitcoin blockchain via the Ordinals protocol. The artwork was inscribed in February 2023, only weeks after Casey Rodarmor launched Ordinals, giving the collection inscription numbers #83,522–#111,319 — roughly half the supply sits in the coveted sub-100k inscription range. It is widely described as the first original (non-derivative) 10k profile-picture collection fully inscribed on Bitcoin, a primacy claim contested by Bitcoin Frogs but generally conceded to NodeMonkes on the basis of its earlier inscription dates. Each monke carries traits drawn from four categories (Body, Head, Eyes, Earring); per the official Monkedex, 3,750 monkes have one trait, 2,147 have two, 499 have three, and only 13 carry four.

Although inscribed in early 2023, the collection was withheld from sale for most of that year. The anonymous two-person team — which published no roadmap and took no venture funding — sold 8,000 NodeMonkes in a descending Dutch auction on Magic Eden beginning December 21, 2023: the price opened at 0.21 BTC and dropped 0.005 BTC every six blocks (~hourly) until 8,000 valid bids set a clearing price of about 0.03 BTC (~$1,260). The collection surpassed 500 BTC in trading volume within four days of launch.

NodeMonkes became the flagship PFP of the Ordinals movement during the February–March 2024 Bitcoin rally: weekly secondary volume reached roughly $45 million, cumulative Magic Eden volume hit 2,795 BTC (~$190M) by early March 2024, the floor touched 0.81 BTC (~$52,000, up ~2,600% from mint), and on March 4, 2024 the "Alien Hoodie" NodeMonke #2769 sold for 17 BTC ($1.08M) — the first million-dollar NodeMonkes sale and, at the time, the second-highest Ordinals sale ever. In mid-March 2024 a 53% single-day floor surge briefly pushed the collection's market capitalization to ~$558.9 million, flipping Bored Ape Yacht Club.

🌍 Cultural significance

NodeMonkes is routinely framed as "the CryptoPunks of Bitcoin": an anonymous-team, no-roadmap 10k pixel collection whose value case rests on provenance (sub-100k inscription numbers from February 2023) rather than promises. Its community slogan "Send Nodes" became one of the defining memes of the Ordinals movement. The collection anchored the early-2024 Bitcoin NFT boom — leading all NFT collections on any chain in weekly volume in early March 2024 — and on March 18, 2024 its market cap (~$558.9M after a 53% one-day floor surge) briefly flipped Bored Ape Yacht Club, a symbolic passing-of-the-torch moment for Bitcoin-native collectibles. It has remained the bellwether of the Bitcoin NFT market since: The Block (Oct 2025) noted NodeMonkes volume spikes cluster around Bitcoin all-time highs ($2.99M weekly volume in the October 2025 ATH week) and characterized the collection as a late-cycle sentiment gauge. No celebrity ownership is reliably documented.

Famous rare traits

  • Alienate (Head) ↗ (30 · 0.30% of supply) — Rarest trait in the collection per the official Monkedex; an alien-head homage to CryptoPunks' 9 Aliens, and the trait class behind the top sales.
  • Alien (Body) ↗ (41 · 0.41% of supply) — The collection's grail body type; four Alien-body monkes sold for ~2 BTC each on Jan 3, 2024, and Alien Hoodie #2769 set the 17 BTC collection record in March 2024.
  • Cross (Earring) ↗ (50 · 0.50% of supply) — Rarest of the five earring values (vs. Diamond 144, Gold 409, Silver 798).
  • Alienate (Eyes) ↗ (55 · 0.55% of supply) — Rarest of the 29 eye values (next: Phase 109, Laser 149).
  • Gold (Body) ↗ (102 · 1.02% of supply) — Second-scarcest body after Alien (Wrapped is third at 127).
  • Zombie (Body) ↗ (217 · 2.17% of supply) — CryptoPunks Zombie homage; commands a premium over the ~500-count common bodies (e.g. Brown 558, Grey 557).

  • Auction start date: Dec 21, 2023 (nftnow, Leather) vs Dec 29, 2023 (NFTPriceFloor, symbole.art); Dec 21 is better attested but not settled.
  • Native pixel resolution of the art: 16x16 (Liquidium) vs 28x28 (NFTPriceFloor).
  • Size of the honorary (non-canonical) companion set: 424 pieces per symbole.art vs ~400 per Leather.
  • Disposition of the ~2,000 NodeMonkes outside the 8,000-piece main auction (pre-launch one-off auctions of rare pieces are referenced anecdotally; no full accounting found).
  • Official-site body-trait counts sum to ~9,808 of 10,000; the remainder is unexplained (possibly additional body values not captured in the fetch).
  • The 'first 10k PFP on Bitcoin' claim is disputed by Bitcoin Frogs (launched March 2023 but with later inscription numbers).
