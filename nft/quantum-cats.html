📖 Overview

Quantum Cats is a collection of 3,333 Ordinals inscriptions on Bitcoin created by Taproot Wizards, the Bitcoin-native NFT venture co-founded by Udi Wertheimer and Eric Wall, with artwork led by artist and engineer Francisco "FAR" Alarcon. Announced on January 12, 2024, the collection was conceived explicitly as a marketing and funding campaign to revive OP_CAT, a Bitcoin script opcode (short for "concatenate") present in Satoshi Nakamoto's original code but disabled in 2010, whose reactivation proponents argue would enable covenants, smart contracts, and bridges on Bitcoin. The cat imagery is a deliberate play on both the opcode's name and Schrodinger's cat: the official site frames the cats as existing in a "quantum realm," and the artwork itself changes state over time.

The collection's signature technology is "Evolving Inscriptions," billed as the first Ordinals collection whose art evolves on-chain. Multiple future versions of each cat were inscribed ahead of time in encrypted form (roughly 10MB of on-chain data spanning about 10 Bitcoin blocks, at an inscription cost reported around $66,000), with evolutions committed in advance so they unfold in predetermined ways as the OP_CAT proposal process advances. Per a technical write-up by Taproot Wizards CTO "Rijndael" in Bitcoin Magazine, each cat is a tiny (~109-byte) inscription that references a shared "Dispatcher" inscription for rendering logic, which in turn reads "Layer Connector" inscriptions mapping each cat to artwork assets across 40 art layers (backgrounds, body, eyes, mouth, etc.); artwork is encrypted per-layer and decryption keys are only revealed when presigned Layer Connector transactions are broadcast, hiding future traits and rarity until reveal.

The launch was one of the most-watched -- and most chaotic -- events of the early Ordinals era. The one-of-one "Genesis Cat" by FAR sold first, fetching 6.31 BTC (about $254,000, roughly 12x the high estimate) in Sotheby's "Natively Digital" curated Ordinals auction that closed January 22, 2024. The main sale of about 3,000 cats at a fixed 0.1 BTC (~$4,265) each began January 29, 2024, but was halted mid-sale by severe technical failures and postponed three times before completing on February 5, 2024, when the collection sold out for roughly $13 million -- most units going to whitelisted buyers in a five-hour exclusive window, with 313 cats selling in the first two seconds of the public phase. Within a day, the floor price on Magic Eden had more than doubled to 0.243 BTC.

Taproot Wizards had raised $7.5 million in seed funding in November 2023 (led by Standard Crypto, with StarkWare, UTXO Management, Bitcoin Frontier Fund and others) ahead of the drop. The campaign's political goal saw a milestone in April 2024 when the OP_CAT reactivation proposal received an official Bitcoin Improvement Proposal number, BIP-347 -- after the community, egged on by the Quantum Cats project, had tried to meme it into existence as "BIP-420." As of the most recent verified reporting, OP_CAT remains a proposal under debate rather than an activated Bitcoin upgrade.

🌍 Cultural significance

Quantum Cats sits at the center of the 2023-2024 Ordinals movement that brought NFT-style collectibles -- and a bitter culture war -- to Bitcoin. Wertheimer and Wall, already famous as provocateurs for the original Taproot Wizards inscriptions, positioned the collection as more than art: it was an activism vehicle, using collectible sales to bankroll and popularize a soft-fork lobbying effort (OP_CAT / "BIP-420") -- a novel model where a meme collection doubles as protocol politics. The Sotheby's sale of the Genesis Cat at 12x its estimate was widely covered as a legitimization moment for Bitcoin-native digital art.

The mint itself became part of the lore: three public delays and a halted sale made headlines as an "embarrassment," yet the collection still sold out for ~$13M and immediately traded at multiples of mint, dominating Ordinals secondary volume in the following days. The "Evolving Inscriptions" mechanism -- encrypted, pre-committed art that changes as the OP_CAT campaign progresses -- is also cited as a genuine technical contribution to on-chain art on Bitcoin.
