📖 Overview

Doodles is a collection of 10,000 generative profile-picture (PFP) NFTs on Ethereum, launched on October 17, 2021 at a mint price of 0.123 ETH. The art — pastel-toned line characters including humans, cats, aliens, apes, pickles and skeletons — was created by Canadian illustrator Scott Martin, known as Burnt Toast. The collection sold out quickly and its floor price climbed to 2.3 ETH within the launch month; current supply is tracked at 9,998 items.

The founding team paired Martin's art with two NFT-industry veterans: Evan Keast ("Tulip") and Jordan Castro ("Poopie"), both formerly of Dapper Labs, where they worked on CryptoKitties. The launch was notable for its community strategy — the pre-mint Discord was closed at an early member cap, with existing members receiving mint access — and for DoodleBank, a community treasury (later reported above $5 million) through which holders vote on proposals. Trait rarity was augmented by 62 fully hand-drawn "Custom" Doodles by Martin, the collection's closest analogue to CryptoPunks' Aliens or BAYC's solid-gold fur.

Doodles became one of the cycle's "blue chip" PFP collections and then deliberately repositioned as a media/consumer brand. Extensions include Space Doodles (February 2022, a reversible wrapper placing each Doodle in an animated spaceship) and the Dooplicator (May 2022, a device for generating wearables tied to Doodles 2). Pharrell Williams joined as Chief Brand Officer in June 2022, and in September 2022 the company raised $54 million at a $704 million valuation, led by Alexis Ohanian's Seven Seven Six with FTX Ventures, Acrew Capital and 10T Holdings. In 2023 a co-founder publicly stated Doodles was "no longer an NFT project"; later milestones include a McDonald's McCafé holiday-cup collaboration (November 2024) and the launch of the DOOD token on Solana (May 9, 2025, 10 billion supply with roughly 30% airdropped to the community).

🌍 Cultural significance

Doodles is regularly grouped with CryptoPunks, Bored Ape Yacht Club and Pudgy Penguins among the definitive 2021-cycle PFP collections, distinguished by Burnt Toast's pastel aesthetic and an early community-treasury governance model (DoodleBank). Celebrity holders reported in the press include Steve Aoki (who bought the second-most-expensive Doodle) and Snoop Dogg, with DJ Timmy Trumpet also cited as an owner. Pharrell Williams' appointment as Chief Brand Officer in June 2022 signalled a push into music and entertainment, followed by the $54 million Seven Seven Six-led raise at a $704 million valuation in September 2022. The brand's stated pivot away from being purely an NFT project (a co-founder told CoinDesk in March 2023 it was "no longer an NFT project") drew both mainstream partnerships — most visibly the November 2024 McDonald's McCafé holiday-cup collaboration, which drove a sharp floor-price and volume rebound — and criticism from crypto-native collectors. In 2025 the brand launched the DOOD token on Solana (10 billion supply, ~30% community airdrop), which more than doubled NFT sales volume around launch.

Famous rare traits

  • Custom hand-drawn Doodles ↗ (62 · 0.62% of supply) — One-off pieces hand-drawn by Burnt Toast rather than generated; each also received a manually assembled, extra-animated Space Doodle. Breakdown: 5 Mascots, 10 Aliens, 12 Cats, 15 Skellys, 20 Apes.
  • Mascot (custom type) ↗ (5 · 0.05% of supply) — Rarest character type in the collection (0.05%).
  • Alien (custom type) ↗ (10 · 0.10% of supply) — Second-rarest character type (0.1%).
  • Ape (custom type) ↗ (20 · 0.20% of supply) — Includes the one-of-a-kind gold ape combination on Doodle #6914, the collection's record sale.
  • Rainbow Puke (face) ↗ (~118 (1.18%) · 1.18% of supply) — The most iconic generative face trait; count inferred from the published 1.18% rarity figure, not an exact stated edition count.
  • Reported 1/1 generative traits ↗ (1 each (reported) · 0.01% of supply) — Traits such as Stellar Sweater, Devil Cat and Holographic Alien are reported to exist in single quantities (NFTevening); not independently confirmed on-chain here.
