📖 Overview

Pudgy Penguins is a collection of 8,888 profile-picture (PFP) NFTs on the Ethereum blockchain, each depicting a cartoon penguin assembled from roughly 150 hand-drawn traits. The collection minted on July 22, 2021 at 0.03 ETH (about $90) and sold out in under 20 minutes. It gained early mainstream attention when New York Times columnist Kevin Roose featured the project in an August 2021 column ("I Joined a Penguin NFT Club Because Apparently That's What We Do Now"), one of the first major-press treatments of "community NFT" culture.

The project was founded by a group of university students led by Cole Villemain (ColeThereum), with co-founder Clayton Patterson ("tubbyfat") and two pseudonymous collaborators. In January 2022 the community voted on Discord to oust Villemain and Patterson amid accusations of unmet roadmap promises and treasury mismanagement. In April 2022 Los Angeles entrepreneur Luca Netz (legal name Luca Schnetzler) acquired the project and its IP for 750 ETH (~$2.5 million) and refocused it as a consumer brand; the collection is operated by Igloo Inc.

Under Netz, Pudgy Penguins became the most prominent example of an NFT project crossing into mainstream retail: a physical toy line (Pudgy Toys, launched May 2023 with PMI) entered over 2,000 Walmart stores and later Target, Walgreens and Amazon, selling more than a million units and over $10 million in revenue in roughly its first year, with each toy unlocking a digital "Pudgy World" experience. In December 2024 the brand launched the PENGU token on Solana (88.88 billion supply, with ~25.9% airdropped to the community), and its parent company built Abstract, a consumer-focused Ethereum layer-2 network launched in January 2025. The ecosystem also includes Lil Pudgys (22,222 companion NFTs, December 2021) and Pudgy Rods.

🌍 Cultural significance

Pudgy Penguins is widely cited as the NFT project that most successfully escaped the 2022 crypto downturn by becoming a mainstream IP brand. After the August 2021 New York Times column and a February 2022 Sotheby's auction ("Snowed In: A Rare Pudgy Penguins Sale", 10 NFTs for ~$129,000 total), the Netz era turned the penguins into a licensed consumer product: Walmart shelf space in 2,000+ stores (later 3,000+ plus Target, Walgreens, Amazon and Chuck E. Cheese), over a million toys sold, and official GIF stickers with tens of billions of GIPHY views (reports range from ~28.9B to 39B+), making the penguins a default reaction meme far beyond crypto. The December 2024 PENGU airdrop pushed the NFT floor above $100,000 (a 26.55 ETH / ~$101K sale), briefly pricing one penguin above one Bitcoin. Documented notable holders include Mark Cuban (sold his for over $30,000 in 2025 per The Block) and rapper Tory Lanez (bought in 2022); the January 2022 founder-ouster-by-Discord-vote and subsequent $2.5M community-driven sale remain a frequently referenced case study in NFT project governance.

Famous rare traits

  • Green Background ↗ (1 · 0.01% of supply) — Unique to Pudgy Penguin #6873 — the only green-background penguin in the collection (0.01%).
  • Black Skin ↗ (1 · 0.01% of supply) — Unique to #6873 (0.01%).
  • Mirrored Body (left-facing) ↗ (1 · 0.01% of supply) — #6873 is the only penguin in the 8,888 that faces left; this single trait drove its record sales.
  • Trick Or Treating Background ↗ (~1 (0.01%) · 0.01% of supply) — Carried by #5678, the second-rarest penguin, which has four one-of-a-kind traits.
  • Pillow Case Body ↗ (~1 (0.01%) · 0.01% of supply) — Also on #5678 (0.01% per Rarity Sniper).
  • Four-trait penguins ↗ (~249 (2.8%) · 2.80% of supply) — Only 2.8% of the collection has just 4 traits; the other 97.2% have 5 — fewer traits reads as rarer.
