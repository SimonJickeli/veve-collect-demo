📖 Overview

VeeFriends is an Ethereum NFT project created by entrepreneur Gary Vaynerchuk ("GaryVee"), minted on May 11, 2021. The original collection (Series 1) comprises 10,255 ERC-721 tokens depicting 268 characters hand-drawn by Vaynerchuk — animals personifying traits he admires (Empathy Elephant, Patient Panda, "Diamond Hands" Hen). Tokens were sold through a roughly three-week Dutch auction (admission tokens settling around 0.5–3 ETH; access tokens listed as high as 49 ETH) and sold out by June 1, 2021; Vaynerchuk retained 1,242 tokens, later badged "GOO" (Gary's Original Ownership). Reported launch proceeds exceeded $20 million.

The project's defining pitch was utility over art: every Series 1 token doubled as a three-year ticket (2022–2024) to VeeCon, Vaynerchuk's annual conference, and the supply was split into three functional tiers — 9,400 Admission tokens (235 characters x 40 editions), 300 Access tokens redeemable for experiences with Vaynerchuk (32 characters: 165 group, 108 one-on-one, 22 competition, 5 scholarship), and 555 Gift Goat tokens carrying recurring physical gifts. This structure made VeeFriends one of the most-cited early examples of "utility NFTs."

Series 2 followed in April 2022 on a separate contract: 55,555 generative tokens across 251 characters (236 returning plus 15 new), each rendered in one of 8 poses against one of 74 scenes, minted at 0.335 ETH (about $995 at the time), with allocation mechanics tied to the Book Games burn token from Vaynerchuk's book "Twelve and a Half." The brand has since pushed heavily into physical licensing — Christie's-auctioned original drawings, Macy's and Toys "R" Us collectible lines (2022), zerocool/Topps trading cards, and Squishmallows and Mattel Creations collaborations (2025).

🌍 Cultural significance

VeeFriends is the flagship of the "utility NFT" thesis and the vehicle for VeeCon, billed as the first NFT-ticketed super conference: the inaugural edition at U.S. Bank Stadium, Minneapolis (May 19–22, 2022) was open only to the 10,255 Series 1 token holders and featured Beeple, Deepak Chopra, Randi Zuckerberg, Logan Paul, Eva Longoria and Pharrell Williams, with Snoop Dogg, Spike Lee and Mila Kunis among attendees; later editions ran in Indianapolis (2023, ~5,000 attendees) and Los Angeles (2024), with no 2025 edition held. At Christie's in October 2021 five of Vaynerchuk's physical source drawings brought $1.26M — coverage noted the lots outsold Warhol and Pollock works in the same auction — and secondary NFT volume was reported above $90M within the first five months. The project's Discord was described as the largest NFT community server (~341k members, April 2022). VeeFriends has since become a licensing brand: Macy's and Toys "R" Us collectible figures (2022), zerocool/Topps trading cards, and 2025 Squishmallows and Mattel Creations collaborations.
