📖 Overview

Meebits is a collection of 20,000 procedurally generated 3D voxel-style avatars on Ethereum (ERC-721), created by Larva Labs — the two-person studio behind CryptoPunks and Autoglyphs. Announced and launched on May 3, 2021, Meebits was pitched as the 3D successor to CryptoPunks: low-poly characters in the Minecraft/Roblox visual tradition, intended for use as avatars in "virtual worlds, games, and VR." Each owner can download an asset pack including the full 3D model and a T-pose OBJ file for rigging and animation, and the project shipped with a built-in zero-fee marketplace supporting ETH sales and multi-item Meebit-for-Meebit trades signed off-chain.

Distribution split the supply in two: roughly 11,000 Meebits were reserved as free claims (gas only) for CryptoPunks and Autoglyphs owners during a 7-day community grant, while the remaining ~9,000 were sold via Dutch auction starting at ~2.5 ETH. The public sale sold out within hours, grossing on the order of $70–80 million (~22,500 ETH). Launch day also produced one of the NFT era's best-known exploits: because trait data had leaked via IPFS, an attacker wrote a contract that repeatedly "rerolled" mints — reverting any transaction that didn't yield a rare ID — and landed Meebit #16647, an ultra-rare Visitor, which collector Pranksy bought for 200 ETH (~$700,000). Larva Labs briefly paused minting in response.

In March 2022, Yuga Labs (Bored Ape Yacht Club) acquired the Meebits and CryptoPunks IP from Larva Labs — along with 1,711 Meebits held by the studio — and extended full commercial rights to individual holders. In February 2025, Yuga sold the Meebits IP onward to The Meebit Company (MeebCo), a firm backed by venture fund 1confirmation and led by Sergio Silva, which announced a "Bits" participation-rewards system and an OpenSea marketplace partnership.

🌍 Cultural significance

Meebits carries cultural weight primarily through lineage: it was Larva Labs' third project and the designated 3D heir to CryptoPunks, launched at the peak of 2021 NFT mania with the then-novel "free claim for holders of the ancestor collection" model that many later projects copied. It became an early flagship of the metaverse-avatar thesis (owner-downloadable, riggable 3D models), and its launch-day reroll exploit remains a canonical case study in NFT smart-contract security. The collection was also the poster child of the January 2022 LooksRare wash-trading episode (~$4.4B in fake Meebits volume), a defining event in NFT market-data skepticism. Its IP odyssey tracks the industry's consolidation arc: Larva Labs → Yuga Labs (March 2022, with commercial rights granted to holders) → The Meebit Company/MeebCo (February 2025, backed by 1confirmation). Prices never sustained CryptoPunks-level demand — MeebCo's CEO called the avatars "very ahead of their time" — but the collection remains one of the most historically significant profile-picture projects on Ethereum.

Famous rare traits

  • Dissected type ↗ (5 · 0.03% of supply) — Rarest type: anatomical cross-section characters; DappRadar put their average sale price at 700 ETH. The Meebits analogue of CryptoPunks' 9 Aliens.
  • Visitor type ↗ (18 · 0.09% of supply) — Green-skinned aliens, an homage to CryptoPunks' Alien punks. Both headline early sales (#8598, #16647) were Visitors; average sale price ~330 ETH per DappRadar.
  • Skeleton type ↗ (57 · 0.29% of supply) — Third-rarest type.
  • Robot type ↗ (72 · 0.36% of supply) — Fourth-rarest type.
  • Elephant type ↗ (256 · 1.28% of supply) — Fifth-rarest type.
  • Pig type ↗ (711 · 3.55% of supply) — Second-most-common type; the remaining 18,881 Meebits are Humans.
