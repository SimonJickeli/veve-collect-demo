📖 Overview

DeGods is a 10,000-piece profile-picture (PFP) NFT collection depicting "degenerate" deities in modern streetwear, launched on the Solana blockchain on October 8, 2021 at a mint price of 3 SOL (~$450). The mint sold out in seconds — largely to bots due to a technical error — and early missteps briefly earned the project rug-pull accusations before it recovered to become the highest-valued NFT collection on Solana. Its early design included the "Paper Hands Bitch Tax," a 33.3% penalty on sales below the floor price; 535 DeGods were burned under this mechanism, cutting supply to roughly 9,465.

The project built an ecosystem around the collection: the $DUST staking token (33.3M max supply), the DeadGods art upgrade (March 2022, unlocked by burning 1,000 $DUST), the 15,000-piece sister collection y00ts (September 2022), and the DeDAO treasury/governance arm. In December 2022 the team announced it was leaving Solana; DeGods migrated to Ethereum in April 2023 (contract 0x8821bee2ba0df28761afff119d66390d594cd280), while y00ts moved to Polygon under a $3M grant and then to Ethereum in 2023–24, returning the grant. In February–March 2023 the 535 burned DeGods were re-inscribed on Bitcoin via the Ordinals protocol — all in a single block, #776408 — making DeGods one of the first major brands present on Solana, Ethereum, and Bitcoin.

The collection was created by Rohun Vora, known as "Frank" (frankdegods), through the Los Angeles studio De Labs (which merged with Dust Labs; Kevin Henrikson was co-founder). The August 2023 "Season III" update — a self-described "art downgrade" — triggered a community backlash that halved the floor price within days. Vora stepped down as CEO on May 12, 2025, handing leadership to community members @0x_chill and @pastagotsauce.

🌍 Cultural significance

DeGods is the defining Solana NFT success story and one of Web3's best-known comeback narratives: pre-mint hype built on the provocative 33.3% "Paper Hands Bitch Tax," a botched bot-dominated mint, near-collapse in late 2021, then a 2022 run to the top of the Solana market (monthly volume grew from ~$375K in Dec 2021 to $25M+ in April 2022) powered by the DeadGods deflationary art upgrade and $DUST staking. In April 2022 its DAO made mainstream headlines as the first NFT community to buy into a professional sports team, paying ~$625,000 (via 25 Fire-Tier BIG3 NFTs) for ownership of the Killer 3s in Ice Cube's BIG3 league — Ice Cube himself bought DeGod #3177 for 265 SOL (~$26,500). The project's serial chain-hopping (Solana → Ethereum, y00ts to Polygon and back, 535 burned pieces inscribed in a single Bitcoin block) made it a bellwether for multi-chain NFT strategy, while the August 2023 Season III "art downgrade" — which halved the floor within days — became a cautionary tale about altering PFP art. Founder Rohun Vora ("Frank"), one of the most-followed and most-scrutinized figures in NFTs, denied insider-trading allegations and retired from the project on May 12, 2025.
