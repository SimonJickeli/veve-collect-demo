How these numbers are estimated. VeVe does not publish what you paid, so: cost = the item's market median in the month you received it (items received from VeVe itself are counted at drop price); proceeds = the median in the month it left your wallet; value now = trailing-30d median of real sales, else the cheapest live listing. Transfers to the burn wallet count as $0 proceeds. Comics are counted but not valued — there is no public comic sale feed, and a name-coincidence match would invent numbers. Everything marked est comes from market data, not your receipts. Not financial advice.
