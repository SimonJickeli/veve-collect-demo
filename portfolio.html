Portfolio PNL

Paste your VeVe wallet address — every buy, sell and burn is reconstructed from the chain and valued against real market sales at the time. All-time profit & loss, estimated honestly.

🔒 Who can see this? Only someone who knows the wallet address. Your 0x address is shown only to you inside the VeVe app (Wallet → Send/Receive OMI → Receive) — there is no public way to look up an address from a username, and this page adds none: no address lists, no name linking, no counterparties shown, and nothing you enter here is stored or sent anywhere except the public chain explorer. Proper account login arrives with StackR integration.

How the numbers are estimated — VeVe does not publish what you actually paid, so: · cost = the item's market median in the month you received it (chain timestamp × real sales history; items received from VeVe itself are counted at drop price); · proceeds = the market median in the month it left your wallet; · value now = trailing-30d median of real sales, else the cheapest live listing. Comics have no public sale feed here — they are counted but not valued (no invented numbers; comic totals arrive when a comic price source exists). Transfers to the burn wallet count as $0 proceeds. Everything marked est is an estimate from market data, not your receipts. Not financial advice.
