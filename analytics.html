Concept prototype. A proposal for what StackR could be once trading moves to Collect — built by the VeVe Collect community toolset on real data. Not an official StackR, VeVe or Collect page.

How the numbers work. Every value is computed from completed sales on both venues (VeVe gem market, StackR OMI market, auctions) since July 2025 — never from asking prices. A single sale that prints far above an item's normal level (a mint #1 flip, a wash trade) is excluded from “typical” prices and still shown as a record. Asking prices appear only as asks, clearly labelled. Not financial advice.
