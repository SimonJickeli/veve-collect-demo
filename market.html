How these numbers work — in plain words:
· Everything is computed from real sales only (both markets, since 2025-07-12) — never from listing/ask prices.
· Premium-mint sales are excluded: when a collectible's price suddenly prints several times its normal level on a single sale (a mint #1 flip, a wash trade), that sale doesn't count. The index tracks ordinary floor-tier items — what a normal collector actually holds.
· 👤 Collector view answers: "if I'd bought one of every traded item at its first sale, how am I doing?" 🏦 Whole market weights items by size, so big editions dominate.
· Marketcap = the item's typical traded price over the last 30 days × copies in circulation. Items with no sale in 14 days don't count — we won't value a whole edition off an old print. And when an item has under 3 recent sales but a cheaper live listing, the listing price wins — a special-mint sale can't set the value of something you can buy for less right now. Top collectibles additionally need ≥3 trades in 30 days to rank.
· Typical item = the MIDDLE item's change (half the basket did better, half worse), and breadth = the share of the basket actually up since entry. Read them together with the headline: a big index number next to low breadth means one item carried the basket, not that the whole franchise rose.
· 🛒 from $ = the cheapest live listing in that basket right now (an entry price, never used in the index math). ⚠ few trades = under 12 traded items, take the curve with a grain of salt. Not financial advice.
