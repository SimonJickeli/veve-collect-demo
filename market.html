Method: each index is equal-weight, entry-at-first-trade — buy 1 of every collectible in the basket at its first traded price (since 2025-07-12), track the portfolio vs cost, rebased to 100. Daily price = median of that day's real sales (both markets); days without a trade carry the last price forward. Marketcap = trailing-30-day median traded price (else last sale) × editions in circulation. Small baskets (few members) swing hard — check the members count. Not financial advice.
